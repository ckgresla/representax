* E5-V upstream compatibility finding

E5-V records Sentence Transformers 5.4.0 and Transformers 5.5.0 in its source
artifact.  That pair loads all 686 encoder tensors and is the numerical oracle.
The latest Sentence Transformers 5.6.1 delegates feature extraction to the new
Transformers 5.6 base =LlavaNextModel=.  Its renamed vision prefix reports all
24 source vision layers as unexpected, creates 24 missing randomly initialized
layers, and therefore cannot serve as a valid oracle.  Representax accepts both
legacy and current Safetensors prefixes explicitly and never silently accepts a
partially initialized model.

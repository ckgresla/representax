* Decision

ModernVBERT text depth now lowers to one =lax.scan= over a depth-major Equinox
PyTree, with a =jax.checkpoint(..., policy=nothing_saveable)= scan body.  The
native tree has exactly the upstream trainable parameter count: layer zero has
no synthetic attention-norm parameter, while the remaining 21 norms are stored
as their own depth-major stack.  Per-layer scalar leaves retain the checkpoint's
full/sliding attention schedule.

This removes the pathological direct-training compilation boundary.  Before the
change, batch 10 at sequence 512 remained in XLA GPU compilation after several
minutes, its post-rematerialization HLO grew to 103,205 lines, and XLA planned
20.17 GiB.  The scanned program completes compile plus first execution in 29.18
seconds with a 4.536 GiB allocator peak.

At the matched batch-8 point, the scan reduces allocator peak by 74.5% and
compile plus first execution by 51.8%.  Throughput is 24.3% lower because
=nothing_saveable= rematerializes every transformer block during backward.  The
result is therefore a correct, bounded baseline rather than the final execution
policy.  Selective residual checkpointing can now be evaluated without changing
the stacked parameter representation or scan control flow.

That follow-up is complete in =../modernvbert-rematerialization-20260813/=: the
small selective speed gain nearly doubles matched memory and reduces observed
batch capacity, so full rematerialization remains the measured default.

* Correctness contract

- the native fast suite asserts one depth-length scan containing one rematerialized
  body;
- Hugging Face load/export round trips every upstream parameter and asserts the
  native trainable parameter count is exact;
- pinned Transformers 5.3 forward and input-gradient parity passes in FP32 at
  highest matmul precision.  Hidden states have max absolute error =2.04e-4= and
  relative L2 =3.54e-6=; pooled embeddings =7.75e-7= and =3.18e-6=; input
  gradients =3.95e-5= and =4.16e-5=.  All three cosine similarities report
  =1.0=; and
- direct and GradCache compiled training updates remain finite.

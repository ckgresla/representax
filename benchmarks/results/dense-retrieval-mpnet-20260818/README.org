The cached plan amortizes its compilation cost after approximately 171 updates.

is both slower and materially larger, whereas exact GradCache changes the
feasible workload and reverses the sustained-throughput result.

* Interpretation and remaining gate

This independently replicates the cached-training advantage on a second native
architecture family. It does not establish a quality advantage: each row is one
seed and the two frameworks do not share an identical dropout RNG stream.
Multi-seed time-to-quality, direct/cached OOM frontiers, and the end-to-end
ModernVBERT replication remain required before closing the dense-training proof.

* Three-run cached replication

The accepted batch-128/chunk-8 plan was repeated with explicit paired seeds 17,
42, and 73. Representax sustains 111.1 versus 95.8 examples/s on average, a mean
=1.160x= speedup with a small-sample 95% Student-t interval of
=[1.156, 1.163]=. All three runs pass exact initial evaluator parity and favor
Representax individually. Compilation amortizes after 232 updates on average,
with interval =[214, 250]=.

The mean final nDCG@10 difference is =+0.01302= with interval
=[-0.01633, +0.04238]=, so the endpoint evidence supports no quality advantage
or regression. Exact inputs, hashes, values, and intervals are in
=three-seed-aggregate.json=.

ModernVBERT, fixed-checkpoint time-to-quality curves, and direct/cached OOM
frontiers remain before closing the parent roadmap item.

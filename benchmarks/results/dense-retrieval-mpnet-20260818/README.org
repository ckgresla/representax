The cached plan amortizes its compilation cost after approximately 171 updates.
The 100-step wall-clock job remains slower before that crossover. Direct MPNet
is both slower and materially larger, whereas exact GradCache changes the
feasible workload and reverses the sustained-throughput result.

* Interpretation and remaining gate

This independently replicates the cached-training advantage on a second native
architecture family. It does not establish a quality advantage: each row is one
seed and the two frameworks do not share an identical dropout RNG stream.
Multi-seed time-to-quality, direct/cached OOM frontiers, and the end-to-end
ModernVBERT replication remain required before closing the dense-training proof.

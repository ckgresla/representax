#+title: NQ and MIRACL bounded real-data acceptance - 2026-08-31

* Scope

This is an operational acceptance gate for the Natural Questions and MIRACL
paper rows. It is not a model-quality benchmark. Each row executes exactly two
training updates through =representax.train.run_job=, stops after update 1,
restores the complete Orbax state, runs the configured canonical information
retrieval evaluator on start and end, exports the final native inference
bundle, reloads it, and reruns that same evaluator over the exported model.

All generated data, checkpoints, logs, and exports live below
=/home/ckg/representax-artifacts/nq-miracl-acceptance-20260831/=. No artifact
was written under =/raid=. The NQ source checkpoint was read from an existing
immutable Hugging Face snapshot under =/raid/.cache=; the Jina snapshot was
resolved into the acceptance root's Hugging Face cache.

* Bounded data

Natural Questions used the first 8 real train pairs and 4 test queries with
positive qrels present in a 4 MiB byte range of the pinned corpus. That prefix
contained 7,040 complete documents; only the four selected positives were
materialized for evaluation.

MIRACL used two real train pairs and one real dev query/document per language
for Arabic, English, Hindi, and Japanese. Corpus reads were capped at a 16 MiB
compressed range per language and stopped after 2, 3,110, 130, and 549
documents respectively. The resulting acceptance data contains 8 train pairs
and 4 evaluation queries/documents.

Both midpoint checkpoints exist at =run/checkpoints/1=. Both final bundles
were independently reconstructed from =run/final-model= and evaluated through
=InformationRetrievalEvaluator= via =EvaluationRunner=. Every exported metric
matched the final =run_job= evaluation event within the enforced =1e-7=
tolerance.

The tiny four-document evaluations report perfect Recall@10/100 and nDCG@10.
Those values only prove evaluator and artifact lifecycle execution: with one
positive per query and three cross-row negatives they do not support a quality
claim.

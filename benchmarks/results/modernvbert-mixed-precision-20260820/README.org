#+title: ModernVBERT mixed-precision acceptance — 2026-08-20

* Result

Representax's BF16 mixed policy preserves FP32 master parameters, gradients,
Optax state, representation accumulation, and task losses while using BF16
model computation, activations, and FSDP parameter communication.  On two RTX
4090s, it nearly doubles matched steady-state ModernVBERT training throughput
for both compiler-managed DDP and FSDP.

| strategy | compute | compile (s) | median step (s) | examples/s | peak live JAX / device | executable temporary | final loss |
|----------+---------+-------------+-----------------+------------+------------------------+----------------------+------------|
| DDP      | FP32    |      19.697 |           0.448 |      17.84 |              3.871 GiB |            1.798 GiB |   1.818959 |
| DDP      | BF16    |      30.085 |           0.223 |      35.91 |              3.872 GiB |            1.799 GiB |   1.862296 |
| FSDP     | FP32    |      24.118 |           1.587 |       5.04 |              2.122 GiB |            1.086 GiB |   1.818622 |
| FSDP     | BF16    |      26.121 |           0.806 |       9.93 |              2.052 GiB |            1.015 GiB |   1.862808 |

BF16 improves DDP throughput by =2.013x= and FSDP throughput by =1.970x=.
FSDP BF16 reduces peak live JAX memory by =3.31%= and executable temporary
memory by =6.46%=.  DDP live memory is unchanged because persistent FP32 master
parameters and Adam moments dominate this point.  NVML process peaks remain
approximately 4.9 GiB because CUDA runtime context and allocator pools obscure
the live-buffer difference; the compiled analysis and JAX live allocator peak
are the relevant measurements.

* Matched contract

- checkpoint: =ModernVBERT/modernvbert-embed= snapshot
  =da507113c3fdbc2e49d39c4b0148025c6bd008f9=
- hardware: two NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090 GPUs, compute capability 8.9
- global batch: 8 query/document pairs
- sequence length: 512
- exact GradCache representation microbatch: 2
- optimizer: AdamW, learning rate =1e-5=
- activation rematerialization: full
- buffer donation: enabled
- seed: 1729
- timing: one retained warmup update followed by three synchronized measured
  updates; the table reports the median measured step
- allocator: =XLA_PYTHON_CLIENT_PREALLOCATE=false=

The measured loss trajectories are:

- DDP FP32: =2.038357, 1.943164, 1.818959=
- FSDP FP32: =2.038355, 1.943153, 1.818622=
- DDP BF16: =2.046249, 1.966795, 1.862296=
- FSDP BF16: =2.045780, 1.965065, 1.862808=

DDP and FSDP follow the same trajectory within each precision.  BF16 is not
expected to be bitwise equivalent to FP32; focused direct, accumulated, and
exact-GradCache tests instead enforce bounded forward and gradient error while
requiring all persistent training state and sensitive reductions to remain
FP32.

* Compiled-program evidence

The optimized GPU HLO contains BF16 FSDP parameter gathers, for example one
packed =all-gather-start= carrying matrices including =bf16[2304,768]= and
=bf16[768,768]=.  Corresponding activation and parameter-gradient
=reduce-scatter= payloads use BF16 on the wire, while the result feeding the
optimizer's FP32 master state is converted or accumulated in FP32.  Small
integer masks and task-global FP32 representation collectives intentionally
retain their semantic dtypes.

The compiler-generated collective counts are:

| strategy | compute | all-gather | all-reduce | all-to-all | reduce-scatter |
|----------+---------+------------+------------+------------+----------------|
| DDP      | FP32    |          6 |          7 |          2 |              0 |
| DDP      | BF16    |          6 |          7 |          2 |              0 |
| FSDP     | FP32    |         15 |          4 |          0 |              9 |
| FSDP     | BF16    |         15 |          8 |          0 |              7 |

Counts alone are not a performance contract: dtype, payload shape, fusion, and
scheduling determine communication cost.  The HLO inspection establishes the
requested BF16 payload boundary; physical synchronized timing establishes its
effect.

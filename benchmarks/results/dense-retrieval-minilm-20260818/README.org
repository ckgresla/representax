* Results

Sustained rates exclude Representax compilation and its first execution.
Sentence Transformers has no analogous compiled phase, so its measured trainer
rate is used directly. Process peak is reported as a feasible-workload signal,
not as a penalty for allocator policy.

The cached batch-128 job amortizes compilation after approximately 1,888
updates; cached batch 512 does so after approximately 503. Direct batch 128 is
both slower and much larger than the cached plan, so GradCache is a meaningful
execution choice rather than a universally applied objective variant.

* Data-pipeline finding

With host prefetch deliberately disabled, the direct batch-32 run spent 5.57 of
14.61 post-compile seconds waiting for data and sustained 435.8 examples/s.
Four Grain threads and an eight-batch buffer reduced total data wait to 0.020
seconds and raised sustained throughput to 626.5 examples/s. Placement consumed
0.525 seconds total over 199 measured updates. This is the measured justification
for prefetch; no device-prefetch layer was added.

Local Parquet now uses memory-mapped, bounded row-group reads and does not build
a duplicate Hugging Face Arrow cache before training.

* Interpretation and remaining gate

This establishes exact initial retrieval-metric parity and a first sustained
training advantage for direct batch 32 and cached batches 128 and 512. It does
not yet establish a model-quality advantage: the final nDCG@10 deltas span
=-0.00325= to =+0.01127= in single-seed jobs whose dropout RNG streams are not
identical across frameworks.

Before closing the roadmap item, repeat the meaningful plans for at least three
seeds, add MPNet and ModernVBERT, record direct and cached OOM frontiers, and
report confidence intervals and time-to-quality.

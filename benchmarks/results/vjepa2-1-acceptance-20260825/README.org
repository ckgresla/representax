* Contract
This is semantic acceptance evidence, not a model-quality or throughput claim.
The oracle is the MIT-licensed =facebookresearch/vjepa2= repository at commit
=204698b45b3712590f06245fbfba32d3be539812= and the V-JEPA 2.1 paper
(arXiv:2603.14482).  The frozen tensor fixture has SHA-256
=01d9601da87f785cf26c0a1416d8827165833fe6b4d186e4ce4916c1a6c86719=.

* Accepted evidence
- Official parity: image/video forwards, four-level deep supervision, target and
  context predictions, both L1 reductions, distance weights, representative
  encoder/predictor gradients, one AdamW update, EMA target update, official
  checkpoint conversion, and the float32 bilinear preprocessing kernel.
- Native lifecycle: deterministic finite image/video collation and multiblock
  masks; two compiled disk-to-model updates; asynchronous Orbax checkpointing;
  native export, clean reload, and inference.
- Evaluation: the native model runs through the shared linear-probe, exact
  bounded k-NN, effective-rank, conditioning, and feature-scale evaluator.
- Distribution: a common train step matches the unsharded trajectory under DDP
  and FSDP on two- and four-device meshes.
- Reporting: one physical-GPU W&B acceptance run completed 30 updates, with
  named V-JEPA losses, gradient/update norms, step time, finite-update state,
  and optimizer step emitted through the ordinary asynchronous reporter.

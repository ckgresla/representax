#+title: Native Qwen text-reranker acceptance — 2026-08-22

* Scope

Representax now owns one Torch-free Equinox scoring family for the Qwen2 and
Qwen3 causal decoder layouts used by the official Sentence Transformers
multimodal release.  The accepted physical anchors are:

- =Qwen/Qwen3-Reranker-0.6B@e61197e= (Apache-2.0, tied score rows);
- =mixedbread-ai/mxbai-rerank-base-v2@3ea9d4d= (Apache-2.0, tied score rows);
  and
- =ContextualAI/ctxl-rerank-v2-instruct-multilingual-1b@8fd1edf= as a
  CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0 external compatibility oracle (untied language-model head).

The same reviewed family manifest owns the pinned Qwen3 4B/8B and mixedbread
large architecture metadata.  Those larger capacity points reuse this forward;
they are not misreported here as physically executed checkpoints.

* Contract

The model-associated processor reads each checkpoint's tokenizer, chat
template, default prompt, and left-padding semantics and emits one admitted
finite sequence bucket.  The native forward computes only the final hidden
state and the one or two vocabulary rows consumed by =LogitScore=.  It exposes
raw training logits separately from the checkpoint-configured inference score:
Qwen3 and ContextualAI use identity; mixedbread inherits CrossEncoder's
single-label sigmoid default.

* Numerical and preprocessing parity

The same two query/document records are preprocessed independently by Sentence
Transformers 5.6.1 and Representax. Token IDs and attention masks are exactly
equal for every checkpoint. Full-checkpoint BF16 results on RTX 4090 GPUs are:

| Checkpoint | Output | max abs | relative L2 | cosine |
|-
| Qwen3 0.6B | raw and identity score | 0.134216 | 0.029113 | 0.999577 |
| mixedbread base | raw logit | 0.022205 | 0.002648 | 0.999997 |
| mixedbread base | sigmoid score | 0.003569 | 0.003050 | 0.999996 |
| ContextualAI 1B | raw and identity score | 0.038036 | 0.006174 | 0.999982 |

The BF16 tolerances cover backend reduction order, not a semantic exception:
the tests assert the processor tensors and the raw-versus-activated score stages
separately.

* Training and export

Each physical anchor runs three generic trainer updates with BF16 compute, a
packed INT4 frozen base, FP32 rank-4 LoRA parameters, AdamW, clipping, and a
binary relevance objective over raw logits. All updates are finite and nonzero:

| Checkpoint | losses | update norms |
|-
| Qwen3 0.6B | 0.108025, 0.013862, 0.003361 | 0.117175, 0.129802, 0.103259 |
| mixedbread base | 1.435308, 0.380857, 0.019607 | 0.110094, 0.123625, 0.119469 |
| ContextualAI 1B | 0.017595, 0.011930, 0.005312 | 0.107028, 0.119718, 0.114459 |

For all three anchors, the native adapter exports the exact source Hugging Face
layout plus processor assets, reloads every represented tensor bit-for-bit in
Representax, and reloads in a fresh Sentence Transformers process with exact
preprocessing and accepted logits/scores. The CC-BY-NC-SA weights remain an
external oracle and are never redistributed by Representax.

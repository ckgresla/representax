#+title: Multimodal family acceptance matrix
#+date: 2026-08-22

* Decision

Representax has a physically accepted native implementation for every distinct
backbone, processor, and output-graph family in the official April 2026
Sentence Transformers multimodal checkpoint panel. This is a family-level
claim, not a claim that every parameter-count variant was downloaded and run.
At least one real immutable checkpoint exercises every distinct native forward;
all checkpoint-specific processors and graph forms used by the panel have a
physical anchor.

Larger variants that reuse an accepted forward are listed separately below.
They remain capacity points for the paper's FSDP frontier until physically
executed. Catalogue presence, compatible configuration, and physical
acceptance are deliberately different states.

* Same-forward capacity variants

The following official variants resolve to an accepted native family but were
not all physically executed during this closure run:

- Qwen3-VL Embed and Reranker 8B;
- LCO Omni 7B and E5-Omni 3B/7B;
- BGE-VL large and CLIP ViT-B/16 or ViT-L/14; and
- Qwen3 Reranker 4B/8B and mxbai-rerank-large-v2.

They must not appear as physically benchmarked checkpoints in the paper until
their capacity runs pass. No new model architecture is required for them;
their purpose is to measure the FSDP and feasible-workload frontier.

* Scope after closure

This closes implementation of the official multimodal architecture families.
It does not close the separate paper experiment matrix, the 25-checkpoint paper
panel, full Jina v5 Omni multimodality, or future native audio/video families
chosen independently of the official panel.

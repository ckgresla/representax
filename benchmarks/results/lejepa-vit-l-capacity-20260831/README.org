#+TITLE: LeJEPA ViT-L/16 capacity canary (2026-08-31)

* Status

Accepted for training capacity and steady-throughput planning. This is not an
ImageNet quality result and does not reproduce the upstream 100-epoch run.

* Contract

- Architecture: LeJEPA ViT-L/16, 310,134,784 parameters.
- Objective: two 224px global views, six 98px local views, and 1,024 SIGReg
  slices with the exact paper weighting.
- Precision: FP32 master parameters and BF16 compute.
- Execution: full activation rematerialization and two-device model-axis FSDP
  on physical RTX 4090 GPUs 0 and 1.
- Each fresh process performs three finite updates. Evaluation, checkpointing,
  and export are disabled so this artifact measures training capacity only.

* Result

Global batches 1, 8, and 16 all pass. Batch 16 reaches 5.10 images/s and uses
3.97 GB peak live HBM per GPU. The first compile/update takes 21.66 seconds;
the median of the following two updates is 3.155 seconds.

At that measured rate, 100 full ImageNet-1K epochs project to 7,017 wall-hours,
or 14,035 logical GPU-hours, before evaluation and checkpoints. The paper must
therefore declare a bounded exposure and must not describe it as a reproduction
of the upstream 100-epoch quality result.

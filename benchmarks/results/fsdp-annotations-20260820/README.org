#+title: Annotation-only FSDP acceptance — 2026-08-20

* Decision

Representax now expresses DDP, FSDP, and custom sharding as one global JAX
training program.  =ShardingPlan= declares persistent parameter, Optax-state,
batch, and result layouts.  Shared model primitives annotate exact parameter-use
and activation layouts; JAX/Shardy inserts forward communication and derives its
autodiff transpose.  There is no separate FSDP trainer, per-model materializer,
custom VJP, manual gradient synchronization, or configurable layer boundary.

=AxisType.Explicit= is the trace-time correctness lane.  =AxisType.Auto= remains
the measured execution default.  Tiny ModernVBERT programs match the one-device
loss and every model/optimizer leaf for ten updates on both two- and four-device
meshes.

* Physical acceptance

All physical rows use GPUs 4 and 5, FP32 parameters and compute, highest matmul
precision, AdamW at =1e-5= without clipping or weight decay, exact MNR GradCache,
full activation rematerialization, deterministic synthetic token tensors, and
one warmup plus three synchronized measured updates.

| implementation | parameters | batch / sequence | median step | peak live JAX / GPU | peak process | result |
|----------------+------------+------------------+-------------+---------------------+--------------+--------|
| shared primitive annotations | 149,044,992 | 8 / 128 | 1.477 s | 3.042 GB | 4.937 GB | completed |
| superseded explicit layer control | 149,044,992 | 8 / 128 | 1.333 s | 3.467 GB | 4.941 GB | completed |
| shared primitive annotations | 1,979,520,768 | 2 / 128 | 11.373 s | 21.492 GB | 22.387 GB | completed |
| superseded explicit layer control | 1,979,520,768 | 2 / 128 | 12.524 s | 20.995 GB | — | completed |

At the capacity point, the generic program is 9.2% faster while using 0.497 GB
(2.4%) more peak live memory.  Both GPUs retain exactly 3.959 GB of model state
and 7.918 GB of Adam state, reach 100% sampled utilization, and complete three
finite, accepted updates.  The direct annotations therefore recover the old
layer path's demonstrated capacity without an architecture hook.  The old
1.9105B DDP program still OOMs before this larger FSDP point.

At 149M, exact primitive annotations use less live JAX memory but are 10.8%
slower than the superseded layer control on this PCIe-only topology.  This is a
real schedule-optimization target, not a correctness exception.  Compilation
times are not compared against the old capacity artifact because its executable
was served from a warm persistent cache; the new 1.9795B cold compile took
26.0 seconds.

* Rejected alternatives

- Replicating the complete embedding table reduced collective count but
  regressed the 149M step from 1.477 to 1.730 seconds; distributed gather/scatter
  is cheaper for vocabulary lookup.
- Packing a sliced scan layer and applying a global =jax.reshard= preserved the
  tiny explicit-sharding trajectory, but produced 17 AllGathers, 10 AllReduces,
  6 CollectivePermutes, and a 3.051-second physical step.  It did not recover the
  old schedule and was removed.
- JAX's new reduced/unreduced placement vocabulary did not improve an isolated
  linear transpose and remains absent from public documentation.  Representax
  does not depend on it.

the reviewable decision record; raw local artifacts preserve allocator, layout,

#+title: Native Qwen2-VL family acceptance — 2026-08-22

* Result

Representax now owns one native Equinox implementation for both =qwen2_vl= and
=qwen2_5_vl=. It accepts the linked BGE-VL-Screenshot, Nomic multimodal 3B/7B,
and Jina reranker-m0 artifacts without importing PyTorch or Sentence
Transformers in the training runtime.

| acceptance gate | evidence |
|-----------------+----------|
| tiny same-tensor oracle | Qwen2-VL and Qwen2.5-VL FP32 forward, pixel gradient, every parameter gradient, three-step AdamW update, and HF export/reload pass against Transformers 5.6 |
| BGE-VL-Screenshot | exact processor arrays, native/upstream representation parity, real inference, and three INT4-base/FP32-LoRA updates |
| Nomic multimodal 3B | exact processor arrays, native/Sentence Transformers parity, real inference, imported-LoRA continuation, and three packed-base updates |
| Nomic multimodal 7B | exact processor arrays, native/Sentence Transformers parity, full FP32 two-GPU FSDP inference, and three single-GPU packed-base updates |
| Jina reranker-m0 | exact pair formatting and media arrays, native/Sentence Transformers score parity, real inference, and three adapter updates |

The tiny numerical gates use =4e-5= maximum absolute/relative tolerance for the
forward and pixel cotangent, =7e-5= for parameter cotangents, and =5e-6=
absolute tolerance for updated parameters. Fresh Transformers reload is within
=1e-6= of the original upstream output. The real BF16 checkpoints use cosine
and relative-error gates appropriate to their low-precision upstream programs;
these are parity gates, not downstream-quality claims.

* Three-update training evidence

Every physical probe uses the ordinary Representax =build_train_step= path,
Optax AdamW, finite-gradient acceptance, nonzero accepted updates, and a
post-update inference check.

Loss decrease is only a deterministic optimizer-path sanity check. It is not a
claim about generalization or model quality.

* Systems correction required by the 7B gate

The first Nomic 7B attempt exposed two generic staging errors rather than a
model-specific limitation.

1. Scanned transformer projections were stacked in checkpoint-native
   =[depth, output, input]= layout. XLA hoisted a complete stack transpose,
   including a 3.54 GiB projection. Layer stacks now store the equivalent
   compute-native =[depth, input, output]= layout and convert back only at the
   checkpoint boundary.
2. Passing one full host array to =jax.device_put(..., NamedSharding)= caused
   JAX's internal =_multi_slice= executable to stage all local shards on GPU 0.
   =place_model()= now slices host-resident parameters by
   =addressable_devices_indices_map=, transfers one slice per device, and
   assembles the global array with
   =jax.make_array_from_single_device_arrays=.

With those fixes, full FP32 Nomic 7B inference passes on two RTX 4090 GPUs under
the canonical 90% JAX preallocation pool. A diagnostic 98% pool left
insufficient headroom for NCCL and is intentionally not the accepted setup.

* Reproduction contract

- upstream runtime: Sentence Transformers 5.6.1, Transformers 5.6.0,
  =colpali-engine= 0.3.18, PEFT 0.19.1;
- accelerator isolation: GPUs 4 and 5 only;
- JAX allocator: =XLA_PYTHON_CLIENT_PREALLOCATE=true= and
  =XLA_PYTHON_CLIENT_MEM_FRACTION=0.90=;
- numerical policy: highest JAX matmul precision for parity;
- oracle and JAX accelerator processes are isolated; and
- source revisions are recorded in the model-family manifest.

Raw upstream arrays and diagnostic optimized HLO remain at
=/raid/representax/acceptance/qwen2-vl/=. The tracked tests are the executable
acceptance specification:

- =tests/models/qwen2_vl/test_transformers_parity.py=;
- =tests/models/qwen2_vl/test_real_artifacts.py=; and
- =tests/models/qwen2_vl/test_gpu_acceptance.py=.

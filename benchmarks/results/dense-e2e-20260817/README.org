* Decision

The first complete disk-to-trained-model comparison is scientifically valid but
does not yet satisfy the systems-improvement gate. Representax reproduces the
initial MiniLM STS metric within =7.8e-10= and the Jina Small metric within
=6.1e-6=, trains both native models, publishes inference bundles, and verifies
them in fresh processes. Cold total wall time and memory remain regressions, so
the parent roadmap item stays open.

* Matched contract

- local STS Benchmark JSONL at immutable revision
  =ab7a5ac0e35aa22088bdcf23e7fd99b220e53308=;
- the same ordered rows, fixed-length tokenization, cosine-regression objective,
  AdamW settings, validation set, and start/end evaluation;
- Sentence Transformers =5.6.1=, Transformers =5.3.0=, Torch =2.13.0+cu130=,
  JAX =0.10.1=, separate RTX 4090 GPUs 4 and 5, and one shared host;
- unique cold JAX compilation caches, asynchronous Orbax final checkpoint,
  atomic native export, and fresh-process reload probes; and
- MiniLM: batch 32, length 128, 100 FP32 updates. Jina Small: batch 4,
  length 128, 40 BF16 updates with full activation rematerialization.

The released Jina Sentence Transformers wrapper is inference-only: its forward
uses =torch.no_grad()= and its media auto-detection attempts to download text
that resembles a URL. The matched oracle therefore disables media routing,
deletes unused vision/audio towers, and replaces only that no-grad wrapper with
an autograd-preserving call through the same upstream text model. This adapter
is recorded in =summary.json= and is not presented as an unmodified upstream
training result.

* Result

Times are process wall seconds and memory is process-visible device GiB.

| Model | Runtime | Train/eval/export process | Fresh reload | Total | Device peak | Initial Spearman | Final Spearman |
|-------+---------+---------------------------+--------------+-------+-------------+------------------+----------------|
| MiniLM | Representax | 75.24 | 57.94 | 133.18 | 4.91 | 0.867098 | 0.884405 |
| MiniLM | Sentence Transformers | 14.83 | 17.98 | 32.81 | 2.08 | 0.867098 | 0.885983 |
| Jina Small | Representax | 78.61 | 20.26 | 98.87 | 8.42 | 0.886082 | 0.884123 |
| Jina Small | patched ST oracle | 76.45 | 9.39 | 85.85 | 6.61 | 0.886076 | 0.884352 |

MiniLM spends 30.98 seconds compiling the first training update and 16.74
seconds compiling its evaluator. Jina spends 14.41 and 5.05 seconds
respectively. Jina native evaluation is materially faster after compilation
(13.34/8.64 seconds initially/finally versus 25.56/24.22), but compilation,
checkpoint-template reconstruction during reload, host RSS, and device memory
erase that advantage in the full lifecycle.

Reload verification is exact to FP32 tolerance for MiniLM. Jina's fresh BF16
process has maximum absolute drift =0.001216= and cosine =0.999981=, within the
same explicit BF16 numerical contract used by its upstream parity gate.

#+title: Audio-text retrieval paper preflight - 2026-08-29

* Scope

This is the bounded readiness run for the frozen audio-text retrieval workload.
It uses =LCO-Embedding/LCO-Embedding-Omni-3B-2605@5f6b5329=, 32 ten-second
AudioCaps training pairs, and an AudioCaps text-retrieval panel with 16 audio
queries, 128 text documents, and five relevant captions per query. Both
implementations use cached MNR with scale 20, exact BF16 base weights, rank-4
LoRA on the text tower, cache microbatch 1, global candidate batch 8, four
updates, and seed 7.

Large artifacts are under:

- =/raid/representax/data/audio-text-audiocaps-preflight-v1/=
- =/raid/representax/paper-audio-text-preflight-b8-20260829/=
- =/raid/representax/paper-audio-text-native-1gpu-b8-20260829/=
- =/raid/representax/data/audio-text-audiocaps-scaling-v1/=
- =/raid/representax/paper-audio-text-scaling-20260831/=

The first path contains immutable normalized audio and hashes. The second is
the complete two-GPU Representax and one-GPU Sentence Transformers lifecycle.
The third is the equal-hardware one-GPU Representax timing and resume probe.
The final two paths contain the 64-audio scaling panel and matched one/two-GPU
DDP runs.

* DDP scaling

The accepted weak-scaling probe preserves local batch 32 and doubles global
batch 32 to 64:

| measurement | 1 GPU | 2 GPUs |
|-------------+-------+--------|
| local batch/GPU | 32 | 32 |
| global batch | 32 | 64 |
| warmed examples/s | 2.733419 | 5.241328 |
| throughput ratio | 1.000x | 1.917x |
| scaling efficiency | 100.0% | 95.9% |
| median warm step | 11.7070 s | 12.2106 s |
| final Recall@10 | 0.9125 | 0.9125 |

Each rate is the median over the two warmed updates separated by a forced
midpoint checkpoint/resume. Compilation is excluded. The runs use the same
checkpoint, seed, per-device batch, GradCache microbatch, and data/evaluation
contract. The two-GPU result clears the 90% weak-scaling acceptance threshold.

Representax stopped after update 2 and restored model, optimizer, RNG, and data
state. The complete lifecycle run produced an exact native reload and a verified
merged Hugging Face export. Sentence Transformers also reloaded its midpoint,
final checkpoint, and export exactly.

* Corrections

The preflight found and fixed several issues before the paper campaign:

- audio inputs and multimodal position IDs are now row-major and compatible
  with exact GradCache;
- the LCO default prompt is applied consistently;
- ordinary BF16 LoRA is a first-class training adapter, avoiding the unfair
  comparison of Representax QLoRA against reference LoRA;
- native bundle reload preserves the serialized LoRA adapter type;
- resume restores through a CPU template, avoiding a second full model on GPU;
- callable fingerprints cover the collator itself rather than its entire module;
- completed checkpoints can recover an interrupted export; and
- the reference Arrow dataset returns NumPy audio arrays to the multimodal
  processor;
- DDP now scales the global batch over a real =data= axis instead of reusing
  FSDP capacity sharding; and
- the audio tower applies attention independently to batched chunks instead of
  flattening all chunks into one block-masked sequence. The old formulation was
  numerically correct but introduced unnecessary quadratic work and
  cross-device communication.

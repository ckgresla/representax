#+title: Process-reward preflight evidence

* Result

The bounded Math-Shepherd process-reward preflight passes on one RTX 4090.
Both frameworks use the pinned Qwen3-0.6B backbone and scalar head, global batch
64, four optimizer updates, seed 7, and the same 256 training and 32 held-out
examples.  Every admitted trajectory has four mixed-label steps and fits the
fixed 256-token execution shape under the frozen 2048-token maximum.

The scalar score is supervised directly by binary cross entropy in Representax.
TRL =PRMTrainer= receives the exactly equivalent two-class logits
=[-score/2, score/2]= at the final separator token of every step.

| Metric | Representax | TRL 1.10.0 |
|-
| Initial held-out step accuracy | 0.5234375 | 0.5234375 |
| Final held-out step accuracy | 0.8125000 | 0.8046875 |
| Loss trajectory | 0.9910, 0.8627, 0.5050, 0.4964 | 0.9882, 0.8629, 0.5075, 0.4923 |
| Warm examples/s | 52.7243 | 16.1826 |
| Warm median step seconds | 1.2139 | 3.9504 |
| Compilation plus first execution | 13.5148 s | n/a; eager execution |
| Peak allocated device bytes | 17,946,821,376 | 11,725,405,696 |

Representax is 3.258x faster after compilation.  Its complete preflight takes
longer because asynchronous full-model and optimizer checkpoint flushes dominate
this four-update run.  Checkpoint overhead remains assigned to the later systems
overhead probe; it does not change the warmed training result.

Both sides perform initial/final held-out evaluation, save update 2, resume to
update 4, export, reload, and reproduce their exported probe logits exactly.

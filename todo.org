** Representax paper [6/18] [33%]
  This section is the finite acceptance program for the Representax
  systems whitepaper. Establish numerical and scientific equivalence first,
  then demonstrate statistically supported systems improvements on shared
  workloads and capabilities enabled by native JAX execution. Sentence
  Transformers 5.4 and later support text, image, audio, and video, so those are
  shared comparisons rather than absence claims. The differentiating claims are
  JEPA-style learning, integrated evaluation/checkpoint-safe arbitrary sharding,
  exact GradCache, and a broader native representation-learning task surface.
  Semantics-preserving automatic execution planning belongs to the separate
  Profilax paper below. Keep exactly one =NEXT= item across the repository.
  Arbitrary sharding/FSDP, mixed precision, unified AnyModality execution, the
  multimodal family matrix, late interaction, cross/reranking, reward, LeJEPA,
  distributed evaluation, W&B reporting, and V-JEPA 2.1 are implemented. V-JEPA
  2.1 remains a distinct paper comparison; sparse retrieval is deliberately
  deferred. Freeze only the finite experimental panel, then run the long paired
  evidence campaign; do not pull general development into it.

*** DONE Define measurable monotonic-improvement gates over existing trainers :research:
   This is a paper-protocol decision, not an implementation backlog. Freeze a
   small set of shared training workloads that execute in Representax and a
   maintained incumbent such as Sentence Transformers or PyLate. Give each
   workload its own experiment section and show that Representax preserves the
   intended learning problem and held-out quality while improving the systems
   frontier. Do not combine unrelated tasks into one opaque average.

**** Intended claim
     For a declared shared workload, Representax is a monotonic systems
     improvement when it performs the same scientific training job, preserves
     held-out quality, and then demonstrates one or more of:
     higher sustained training throughput, lower steady-state latency, a larger
     feasible scientific workload, or better accelerator feeding/utilization on
     the same hardware. A capacity improvement means using less live memory for
     the same work or enabling larger models, batches or inputs; efficient FSDP
     and arbitrary sharding are first-class capacity evidence. Compilation is
     disclosed separately and excluded from the primary systems verdict. The
     eight shared sections compare against the relevant maintained incumbent:
     Sentence Transformers where it implements the workload, PyLate for late
     interaction, and task-specific maintained references for reward modeling
     and V-JEPA rather than forcing every claim through one framework.

**** Fair comparison contract
     For each task, use the same initial model, training and evaluation data,
     objective, declared optimization recipe and hyperparameters, precision,
     global batch, update count, evaluation cadence, seeds and hardware in both
     frameworks. The JAX and PyTorch optimizer implementations, sharding,
     rematerialization, prefetching, compilation and kernels may differ because
     those are the systems being compared. Report every seed's loss trajectory,
     held-out quality, training throughput, step time, memory and time to any
     quality target. Compilation, evaluation and checkpoint overhead remain
     separately visible. No separate numerical-parity or dry-run certification
     is required for the paper comparison.

**** Evidence policy
     Use the three paired full-training runs as the independent systems timing
     processes. Record host timings for every step without adding a device
     synchronization to the critical path; use synchronization already required
     by evaluation, checkpointing and run completion to bound completed work.
     Use =perf/tokens_per_second= for non-padding training tokens; keep
     validation quality under =valid/*=.
     Derive warmup exclusions and summaries after collecting the raw trajectory.
     Do not launch duplicate timing-only processes for the primary comparison.
     Short timing-only runs are reserved for 1/2/4-device scaling and capacity
     boundaries that do not warrant full quality trajectories. Retain raw per-run
     values, confidence intervals, negative results, OOMs and no-crossing
     trajectories. Large traces
     and checkpoints live on =/raid=; Git tracks the immutable config, environment
     and device inventory, compact raw metrics, summary, reproduction command and
     scoped verdict. Throughput measured on different hardware or under a
     different scientific objective is contextual evidence, not a paired claim.

**** Model evidence strategy
     Use selected interesting public base checkpoints for realistic fine-tuning
     case studies. Separately train one controlled five-size BERT encoder family
     at approximately 30M, 110M, 500M, 1B and 4B parameters under matched recipes
     to demonstrate that the systems advantage persists with scale rather than
     depending on one architecture or parameter count. The original canonical
     BERT sizes are approximately 110M (Base) and 340M (Large); 30M, 500M, 1B and
     4B are controlled study configurations, not claims of canonical released
     checkpoints. Gate the 4B campaign on a 1B pilot establishing useful training
     coverage and feasible throughput on available hardware. Apply the full size
     ladder to dense retrieval, where training embedding models from scratch and
     fitting larger scientific batches are material use cases. Reranking,
     classification and the other sections use representative architectures
     unless their own result motivates a separate scale study.

**** JEPA task-family decision
     Representax exposes LeJEPA and V-JEPA 2.1 as two explicit selectable tasks
     under one JEPA family, not as modes hidden inside one nominal loss.
     - LeJEPA is the generic lean recipe: predictive agreement plus SIGReg, no
       stop-gradient teacher, no EMA target state and no teacher scheduler. It is
       the simple distributed-friendly representation-learning objective already
       implemented in Representax.
     - V-JEPA 2.1 is the canonical video recipe: spatiotemporal tubelet masking,
       a context encoder and masked-token predictor, stop-gradient targets from
       an EMA encoder, dense prediction and intermediate-layer supervision. It
       receives a direct paired comparison against the official Meta code.
     The tasks share ordinary model execution, mixed precision, sharding,
     checkpointing, reporting and JEPA representation evaluators. Reusable
     V-JEPA mechanisms should be general components where their semantics permit:
     masked-view planning, context/target prediction, EMA state, intermediate
     feature losses and modality tokenizers. They remain composition points, not
     claims that applying every V-JEPA mechanism preserves the LeJEPA objective.
     A later SIGReg-plus-video-prediction composition is a new experimental task,
     not reference-equivalent LeJEPA or V-JEPA 2.1.

*** DONE Measure padding waste before deciding on packing :research:benchmark:data:
   Use ordinary length bucketing for paper training; do not implement GradCache
   sequence packing on the paper critical path. On the four-GPU MPNet/MS MARCO
   pilot, bucketing raised mean token utilization from 17.1% to 32.5% and median
   throughput from 251.80 to 323.65 examples/s (1.285x).

   The matched one-GPU direct-MNR diagnostic proves that packing is scientifically
   valid: real-checkpoint embeddings agree within 9.69e-8, and complete MNR loss
   and parameter gradients match with dropout disabled. A compile-stable fixed
   pack improves fixed-256 throughput 1.240x, but ordinary bucketing improves it
   4.668x and is 3.766x faster than packing. Bucketing uses 4,472 mean token slots
   per update versus 8,640 for the smallest fixed packed ABI covering all 64
   batches. A broad adaptive selector was rejected after causing 13 signatures in
   13 updates.

   Packed repeated evaluation is promising (1.790x final evaluator throughput),
   but it is not needed for the first paper. Frozen image, audio, and video
   workloads already normalize to fixed extents; measure modality-internal mask
   waste in their own preflights before revisiting media packing. Large evidence
   lives under
   =/raid/representax/paper-campaign-v1/preflight/packing/20260828/= and compact
   evidence under =benchmarks/results/paper-packing-preflight-20260828/=.
